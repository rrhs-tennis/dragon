Our Mission

"The goal of the RRHSTB is to promote sportsmanship and further interest in tennis. Members provide their support to the RRHS tennis athletes, RRHS Tennis Coach and school administration. RRHSTB shall strive to provide encouragement and enthusiasm to tennis players at RRHS and surrounding communities. RRHSTB will endeavor to provide its members and the community at large a current view of the RRHS Tennis Program activities and RRHSTB involvement, thereby promoting and enhancing the RRHS tennis program."
